Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 September 2026

Kuvaira respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we may receive when you browse our website, enquire about a gift, or contact us, and how we use it to provide a thoughtful service.

Information we collect

We may receive details you choose to share, such as your name, company, email address, phone number, delivery details, occasion, budget, and message. We also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device, pages visited, and approximate location through standard hosting or analytics logs.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare gifting recommendations and quotations, coordinate fulfilment and delivery, improve the website, prevent misuse, and meet legal or accounting obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and service providers

We may share only the information needed with trusted providers who help us host the website, communicate with you, process payments, prepare gifts, or deliver orders. They are expected to protect information and use it only for the requested service. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights and users.

Cookies and analytics

Our website may use essential cookies or similar technologies for security, performance, and remembering preferences. If analytics or embedded services are enabled, those providers may set their own cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as needed for the purpose collected, our business records, or legal requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online service can promise absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, delete, or stop using personal information, subject to legal and operational limits. You may also opt out of non-essential marketing messages. To make a request, contact Kuvaira through the details on our Contact page and include enough information for us to identify your enquiry.

Children and external links

Our services are intended for adults and businesses, not children. Our website may link to third-party sites; their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The effective date above will show the latest version. If you have a privacy question, please use our Contact page.
