Frequently Asked Questions

Planning a gift that feels personal? Here are answers to the questions we hear most often. If your brief is unusual, tell us what you have in mind.

What does Kuvaira create?

Kuvaira curates premium gifts for employees, clients, milestones, celebrations, and events, with thoughtful packaging and delivery coordination.

Do you handle corporate and bulk gifting?

Yes. We help businesses plan bulk gifting, recipient details, branding, packaging, and delivery around an event or campaign.

Can gifts be customised?

Many gifts can be customised with product choices, notes, packaging, colours, or approved brand elements. Options depend on the brief, quantities, and lead time.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities vary by product and personalisation. Share your approximate quantity and occasion and we will recommend suitable options.

Can you work within a specific budget?

Yes. Tell us your per-recipient or total budget and we will build a considered shortlist with the applicable customisation and delivery costs.

How much lead time do you need?

Lead time depends on quantity, sourcing, personalisation, and destination. Earlier enquiries give us more choice; we will confirm a practical timeline in the quotation.

Do you deliver to multiple addresses?

We can coordinate multi-address delivery for eligible requests. Provide the recipient list and locations early so the delivery plan can be confirmed.

How do I start an enquiry?

Use the Contact page and tell us the occasion, approximate quantity, budget, destination, and deadline. We will respond with next steps and ideas.
